# Room 1: The Four Seals
## Facilitator notes — contains solutions

Audience: teens and adults with no prior fashion-history knowledge.
Estimated first play: 10–15 minutes, depending on reading and exploration.
All necessary evidence is available inside the room. No external research is required.
Wrong answers retain progress. There is no timer or punishment.
The four investigations can be completed in any order.

| Investigation | Hidden object and discovery action | Puzzle | Answer | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The secret beneath the silk | Court gown: lift the loose hem | Identify the support using a three-term dressmaker’s label | Panniers (also accepts pannier, les panniers, side hoops) | Rose = 4 |
| The misplaced waistline | Empire display: unfold the paper beneath its plaque | Compare three illustrated waist positions with a c. 1805 instruction | Sketch A, waist just beneath the bust | Column = 1 |
| Two truths after midnight | Modern dress: slide open the plinth drawer | Select exactly two details supported by a c. 1925 dance card | Dropped waist; shorter hem | Pearl = 2 |
| The signature in the glass | Mirror: polish away the mist | Read a reversed surname alongside the New Look date | DIOR or Christian Dior | Star = 7 |

Each object has an additional inspection action that provides a useful directional nudge.
Discovery changes the 3D scene: a hem label appears, the sketch is exposed,
the drawer slides out, or the mirror signature becomes visible.
The journal records evidence and awarded seal digits.
Inline hints and a global progressive hint system support participants.

## Final ordering puzzle

Hidden location: the invitation's second paper layer, underneath its wax seal.
Action: **Lift the wax-sealed flap**.

> Begin with the pearl that danced. Let the rose of the court follow.
> The star must come immediately before the column.
> Read the four seals from left to right.

Order: **Pearl → Rose → Star → Column**.
Door code: **2471**.

The lock requires all four riddles to be solved and the ordering note to be discovered,
in addition to a correct code. This is educational progression gating, not a security system.
There is no cloud server; answers are necessarily present in the browser bundle.

## Historical grounding

- Court support: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/82640
- Empire silhouette: https://thefidmmuseum.org/learn/articles/empire-era-silhouette
- 1920s evening silhouette: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/156032
- Dior debut / New Look: https://www.galeriedior.com/en/galleries/premier-defile-christian-dior-printemps-ete-1947

The museum references support the historical distinctions. The notes, quotations, seals,
code, and missing collection are fictional teaching devices. Garment models remain stylised
studies. The brief Dior riddle previews, rather than implements, the full planned 1947 room.
