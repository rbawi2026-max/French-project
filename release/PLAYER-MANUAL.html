Quick start

Solo practice → Help me find a clue → Open clue → Find the next clue → Take me to the lock → choose a card → Test the lock → Walk through the door.

Start the complete game

Open the museum and choose Explore in solo practice (or Continue solo practice). This lets you play all four rooms without waiting for 50 people. Read the introduction and select Help me find a clue for the easiest start. Choose Explore the room if you prefer to walk freely. Your solo progress is saved in that browser.

The easiest way to find a clue

  1. Press Find a clue. The camera walks through the room to an object. You can press Stop at any time.
  2. When you arrive, press Open clue. A small gold marker also identifies the object you can inspect.
  3. Watch a short, simple movement: a book opens, a drawer slides, a frame opens slightly, or a garment turns gently.
  4. Read the clue panel. Clue saved in your journal confirms that you found it.
  5. Press Find the next clue, or Keep exploring freely.
  6. Repeat until the counter shows 3 / 3 clues recorded.

The guide helps you reach objects. It does not supply the answer or record a clue before you open it. Your task is to connect what the three clues describe.

Explore freely on a laptop or trackpad

  • W / S: walk forward / backward.
  • A / D: move sideways.
  • Left / right arrow: turn. Up / down arrows also walk.
  • Trackpad: click and hold, then drag to look around. Release before clicking an object.
  • Inspect: click the visible book, frame, drawer or mannequin. If it is too far away, the guide walks you closer. You then press Open clue.
  • E: inspect the nearby object in the centre of the view.
  • J: journal. H: hint. Escape: pause/settings.

Find a clue works with a single trackpad click, so precise aiming and holding movement keys are optional.

Explore freely on a phone

Drag the round thumbstick at the lower-left to walk. Swipe on the room to look around; one finger can walk while another turns the view. Tap nearby objects to inspect. The Find a clue and Open clue buttons provide the same guided option as on a laptop. Landscape gives a wider view; portrait is supported.

If movement feels slow, open the settings menu and select Smooth / phone. You can reduce movement effects and play without sound.

Read, compare and answer

  1. Open Journal to reread discovered clues. Compare lets you select two discoveries and view them side by side. Hint gives a nudge.
  2. After all three local clues, press Take me to the mystery lock. You can also walk to the door yourself.
  3. At the door, press Open the mystery lock. Read the question and your collected evidence.
  4. Tap one answer card, then tap the mystery box. Dragging the card into the box also works. The box displays your selected answer.
  5. Press Test the lock. An incorrect answer keeps the door closed. Compare the clues again before retrying.
  6. In solo practice, a correct answer opens the physical door. Return to the room and press Walk through the door, or walk through manually.

After Room 4, the recovered collection and concluding story appear.

Tournament mode: 50 → 30 → 15 → 5 → 1

  1. Scan the organiser’s QR and choose Join the tournament. Use a unique display name. The event code is filled from the QR.
  2. Wait for the organiser to start Room 1. Each round starts for all eligible players together.
  3. Find the clues and submit the correct answer. Your completion time is recorded on the server.
  4. Wait for everyone to finish, or for the organiser to close the round. Then the leaderboard appears.
  5. The fastest correct 30 qualify for Room 2, then 15 for Room 3, then 5 for Room 4. The final round announces one winner.
  6. Qualified players keep the results screen open until the organiser starts the next room. Other players can watch subsequent results.

The clock continues during reading, menus and hints. Network delays count. If a round closes early, unfinished players do not qualify, and fewer players may advance.

If something seems stuck

  • No clues visible? Clues are inside objects. Press Find a clue, wait until you arrive, then press Open clue.
  • A shelf book does nothing? Some objects are scenery. The guide leads to an interactive object.
  • No movement? Close the clue panel or press Resume. If using guided walking, Stop returns to manual control.
  • The old interface is still showing? Refresh the browser page, then Continue solo practice. Your recorded clues remain saved.
  • The lock asks for more evidence? Check the room counter is 3/3. The final room also requires your earlier discoveries.
  • The tournament will not advance? Wait for the host’s next-round start; only qualifiers continue.
  • Connection lost? Reconnect in the same browser. Recorded tournament results are saved; the timer does not restart.

Access and organiser instructions

This preview runs on the host computer. Other phones must use its Wi-Fi address and share the same network; 127.0.0.1 only works on the computer itself. Keep the server running and the computer awake. Public access over mobile data requires a separate deployment.

The organiser opens the private host link supplied with the project. For a new event, use the host key shown in the server Terminal. Share only the participant QR/link. Start Room 1 after all 50 join, review each results screen, then start the next round. Closing a round early excludes unfinished players.

Historical explanations and sources appear after solving. The missing collection and archive letters are fictional. This manual does not include an answer key.
